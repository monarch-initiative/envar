# Geodata 4 Health (ESIP)

## Goal

Develop data standards for linking geospatial data and clinical data for public health applications. Major objectives include developing "environmental CDEs" and geolocation data standards.

**Website:** [esipfed.org/collaboration-areas/geo-data-4-health](https://www.esipfed.org/collaboration-areas/geo-data-4-health/)

## Status

Active [ESIP](https://www.esipfed.org/) collaboration area. Meets monthly via Zoom and at ESIP meetings biannually (January and July). Resources include a Google Drive, web page, mailing list, Zoom room, and Slack channel in the ESIP workspace — all managed by ESIP.

At the last ESIP meeting, the group held a work-a-thon to brainstorm requirements for environmental CDEs.
