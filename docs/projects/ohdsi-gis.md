# OHDSI GIS Working Group

## Goal

Develop and maintain the geospatial infrastructure for OMOP, including the [Gaia catalog](https://github.com/OHDSI/gaiaCatalog) and the OMOP external exposure table.

**Website:** [ohdsi.github.io/GIS](https://ohdsi.github.io/GIS/)

## Status

Active. Meets weekly. Conversations are often very technical, focused on OHDSI-specific development.

## Connections

- Provides the core infrastructure that the [HIV & Climate Change](hiv-climate.md) project is designed to test
- Goals overlap with [Geodata 4 Health](geodata4health.md) and [EnVar](envar.md)
- EnVar contributes vocabulary and metadata standards back to this group
